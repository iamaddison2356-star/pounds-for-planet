ABOUT POUNDS FOR PLANET

A cleaner planet.
One pound at a time.

Pounds for Planet began with a straightforward idea: pick up trash when there is time, measure the difference, and invite others to support the planet. We plan to start with local beaches, parks, and neighborhoods. Donations are voluntary; they do not set our schedule or create a promise to collect a specific amount of litter.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
pitch in.

Do donations create a cleanup deadline?

No. Cleanups happen when time allows. A voluntary donation does not purchase a specific number of pounds or create a weekly quota.

How will the trash be weighed?

We plan to weigh collected litter, subtract the bag or container weight, and share the date, location, photos, and net weight after each cleanup.

Where does the money go?

We intend to support cleanup supplies and environmental causes. Before donations open, we will name the recipient organizations and explain the allocation, fees, and any project expenses. Transfers and receipts will be shared separately from funds raised.

Can I donate right now?

Donations are not open yet. You can explore a potential gift on our support page, but no payment is collected. A verified donation destination will be connected before fundraising begins.

What does a dollar per pound mean?

It can be a fundraising goal based on litter already removed: 100 pounds collected could inspire a $100 goal. It is not a promise to collect new trash in exchange for your donation.
